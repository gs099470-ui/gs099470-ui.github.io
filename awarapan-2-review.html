Awarapan 2 Review: Emraan Hashmi Returns as Shivam Pandit With a New Chapter

Awarapan 2 has arrived in theatres, bringing Emraan Hashmi back as Shivam Pandit nearly two decades after the original Awarapan. The sequel released on August 14, 2026 and features Disha Patani and Shabana Azmi alongside Hashmi.

Awarapan 2 Cast

  • Emraan Hashmi as Shivam Pandit
  • Disha Patani
  • Shabana Azmi
  • Suvinder Vicky
  • Vijayant Kohli
  • Atul Kumar

What is Awarapan 2 about?

The film continues Shivam Pandit's journey with themes of action, love, sacrifice, revenge and redemption. The story gives the character a new mission while keeping the emotional tone associated with the original film.

Release Date

Awarapan 2 was released in cinemas on August 14, 2026. The film is directed by Nitin Kakkar and produced by Vishesh Bhatt under the Vishesh Films banner.

Review: Is Awarapan 2 worth watching?

Early reviews are mixed but generally highlight Emraan Hashmi's return as the main attraction. Critics have also pointed to the film's nostalgic connection with the 2007 original, while some reviews feel that parts of the screenplay are predictable. For fans of the first film and Hashmi's intense romantic-action style, the sequel offers a strong nostalgia factor.

Music and Nostalgia

The sequel also leans into the musical identity of the franchise. Familiar musical themes and recreated songs are designed to connect the new film with the audience that remembers the original Awarapan.

Awarapan 2 OTT Release

An official OTT streaming date has not been announced yet. The film has first had its theatrical release, and an OTT release is expected after its cinema window. We will update this page when an official streaming date and platform are confirmed.

Final Verdict

Awarapan 2 is primarily aimed at viewers who remember the emotional world of the original film. Emraan Hashmi's return as Shivam Pandit is the biggest reason for fans to revisit the franchise, while the sequel adds a new action-driven chapter. If you enjoy emotional action dramas and nostalgic Bollywood sequels, this one is worth considering.
