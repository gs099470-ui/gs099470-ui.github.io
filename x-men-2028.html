Marvel's New X-Men Movie: Cast, Release Date & Everything Confirmed

Published: August 16, 2026

Marvel has officially revealed the main cast of its new X-Men movie at Disney's 2026 D23 event.

Confirmed release date

The film is currently scheduled for May 5, 2028. Release plans can change.

Confirmed cast

  • Sadie Sink — Jean Grey
  • Kit Connor — Cyclops
  • Christopher Abbott — Professor X
  • Samara Weaving — Emma Frost
  • Maya Boyd — Storm
  • Inde Navarrette — Rogue
  • Adam Driver — Mister Sinister

Who is directing?

Jake Schreier is directing, with Lee Sung Jin and Joanna Calo credited as writers.

Why the announcement matters

The X-Men are one of Marvel's most anticipated additions to the MCU, and the new casting announcement gives the project a much clearer shape.

What we still don't know

Marvel has not revealed the complete plot or every supporting character. More details are expected as production continues.
