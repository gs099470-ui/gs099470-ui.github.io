Cocktail 2 OTT Release: Cast, Story, Release Date & What to Expect

Published: August 16, 2026

Cocktail 2 has moved from the big screen to streaming, giving viewers another chance to revisit its mix of romance, friendship and emotional chaos.

Cocktail 2: Quick Details

  • Cast: Shahid Kapoor, Kriti Sanon and Rashmika Mandanna
  • Director: Homi Adajania
  • Genre: Romance, comedy/drama
  • Theatrical release: June 19, 2026
  • OTT: Netflix; reports list August 14, 2026

What is Cocktail 2 about?

The film follows Kunal and Diya, whose long relationship is shaken when Ally, an old friend, comes back into their lives. The situation quickly becomes more complicated, mixing relationship drama with lighter comic moments.

Who stars in Cocktail 2?

Shahid Kapoor, Kriti Sanon and Rashmika Mandanna lead the film. The supporting cast includes Sanjay Dutt and Tiku Talsania, among others.

Is Cocktail 2 a direct sequel?

It is presented as a spiritual sequel rather than a direct continuation of the original film's characters. The new story focuses on a fresh relationship dynamic.

Final verdict

Cocktail 2 is best approached as a new romantic drama rather than a remake of the original Cocktail.
